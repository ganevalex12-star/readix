Privacy policy for the Readix app

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · applies to Readix for Android from version 3.10. The German version is the binding one.

In short:

  • Readix works without an account. Your books, notes and reading statistics stay on your device.
  • Readix has no ads, no analytics or tracking services and no servers of its own. The developer receives no data from you.
  • Only if you turn on sync does Readix keep a copy in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.

1. Controller

The Readix app is provided by Oleksii Ganiev, Stuttgart, Germany. The postal address and contact details are in the legal notice (Impressum). Please send privacy questions to the email address given there.

2. Data on your device

Readix keeps the following in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • the books you add (files, covers, title, author and other details from the file);
  • your reading position, highlights, notes, quotes and shelves;
  • reading time, reading statistics and reading goals;
  • your name, if you enter it for the greeting, and your settings.

Other apps cannot access this storage. Readix sends this data nowhere except when syncing (section 3). It is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear the app’s data in Android settings or uninstall Readix.

When you open or share a file from another app with Readix (“Open with”, “Share”), Readix copies it into your library. The temporary copy is deleted afterwards.

3. Sync through Google Drive (optional)

When you sign in with Google in Readix, Readix asks Google only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the “drive.appdata” permission). Readix cannot see your other files in Google Drive.

What is transferred: the data from section 2 — book files and covers, book details, reading progress and reading time, notes, highlights, quotes, shelves, reading goals, your name and the accent colour you chose. It travels encrypted (HTTPS) directly between your device and Google. No server of the developer is involved, and the developer has no access to this data.

Your Google account: Google tells Readix the name and email address of your account. Readix keeps them only on your device to show which account is connected. Your password never reaches the app.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — you use sync as a feature of the app. Google processes the data as the provider of your Google account under its own terms and privacy policy. Google LLC (USA) is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA are covered by the European Commission’s adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

Stopping and deleting:

  • “Turn off” sync in Readix settings: Readix hands the access back to Google. The data stays on the device and in Google Drive.
  • “Delete cloud data” in settings: Readix empties its folder in Google Drive. Everything on the device stays.
  • In Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → delete Readix’s hidden app data.
  • You can also remove the access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

4. Google Play and Android

  • Download: when you get Readix from Google Play, Google processes data for this under its own responsibility (your Google Play account, for example). Readix does not receive this data.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on in your Google account, Android may back up Readix’s data (encrypted) and move it to a new device. You control this in your device settings; it is a Google service.
  • Crash reports: Readix contains no crash-reporting or analytics library. If you agreed in Android to send usage and diagnostics data to Google, Google may show the developer aggregated crash statistics in Play Console that do not identify you.

5. What Readix does not do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no profiling, no automated decisions, no selling or sharing of data. Readix needs a single permission: internet access, for sync. Fonts are built into the app, and books cannot load anything from the internet.

6. How long data is kept

On your device and in Google Drive the data stays until you delete it (sections 2 and 3). The developer keeps no data about you.

7. Security

The connection to Google is encrypted. The data sits in the app’s private storage. Access to Google Drive is limited to the app folder, and the access key from Google is kept only in memory. Books cannot run scripts in Readix.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). As the developer holds no data about you, you exercise most of these rights directly in the app or in your Google account. “Save backup” in settings gives you a copy of all your data. For questions, write to the email address in the legal notice.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany, www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de.

9. Children

Readix is not aimed specifically at children. Sync needs a Google account, which is subject to Google’s age rules.

10. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open them, GitHub processes your IP address and technical access data to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub’s privacy statement. The pages set no cookies, contain no scripts and load nothing from other servers.

11. Changes

If the way Readix handles data changes, this policy will be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.
